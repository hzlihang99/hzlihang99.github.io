Hi there! 🙌 I am a second year Ph.D. student in Computer Science studying in Michigan State University (MSU), supervised by [Dr. Jiliang Tang](https://cse.msu.edu/~tangjili). Prior to that, I obtain my Master degress in Statistics from University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) and my Bachelor degree in Applied Mathematics from Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU). My research interests include generative diffusion models, graph learning and AI for Education and get a few works published in conferences like EMNLP, Interspeech, ICASSP, AIED and EDM et al. In addition, I regulary serve as reviewer for some machine learning conference like KDD, AAAI, AIED et al. 

If you want to contact me, please feel free to send email 📧 to lihang4 at msu dot edu or ping me on Linkedin, GitHub and Twitter.
